Lunabotics · Controls

CMU Moon Miners

At CMU’s NASA Lunabotics team, we design and build a lunar excavation robot for competition. I am the Controls Lead, responsible for the embedded systems and control stack. In 2026 we were awarded the best first year team, and were the first ever first year team to score autonomous points.

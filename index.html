Projects

Lunabotics · Controls

CMU Moon Miners

Software Controls Lead on CMU's NASA Lunabotics team, an 80-person team unified in goal of building an autonomous lunar excavation rover. I lead a 20-person software subteam, from GitHub workflows and deadlines to the ROS 2 stack that wires high-level planning down to simulated motor control. We were the first rookie team in Lunabotics history to score autonomous points. I have been integrating LiDAR-based SLAM for obstacle detection and navigation.

Research · Teleoperation

Momentum Robotics Lab

Research assistant on COSTAR, a long-range teleoperation system for multiple 7-DoF arms. I built modular interfaces for iOS motion controls, a web UI, and a GELLO controller, and ran the distributed stack with Zenoh and Docker so other labs could use it. Control software and fail-safes were prototyped in MuJoCo/Robosuite before hardware. I collaborated with a six-person lab team on a paper for IROS 2026.

  • Zenoh · Docker · iOS
  • MuJoCo / Robosuite
  • Jun 2025–May 2026

Personal · Computer Vision

Magic Mirror Optical Illusion

A personal summer project that was mostly an exercise in pinhole camera mathematics, tracking your eyes to make a pseudo-3D effect on a normal screen. I created this as a base for a future Raspberry Pi-based magic mirror design with lifestyle widgets with a 3D background effect.
